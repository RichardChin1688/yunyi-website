Yunyi runs Chinese medicine wellness courses taught by the founder, covering constitution awareness, seasonal dietary practice and everyday care. They are knowledge sharing — not medical advice, and no substitute for a consultation. Recording is in progress; schedule and enrolment are to be announced.
