Policy

How information is handled

This notice explains how the Yunyi official website (yunyi.homes, both the Chinese and English versions) handles information relating to you. The site is operated by Shenzhen Yunming Yuchen Culture Communication Co., Ltd.

1. No personal information is collected. This is a static website: every page is a pre-built HTML file. There are no online forms, message boxes, registration or login features, so no name, telephone number, email address, postal address or other personal information is collected.

2. No cookies and no tracking scripts. The site uses no analytics, advertising, pixels or fingerprinting, sets no cookies and performs no cross-site tracking. Apart from structured data (JSON-LD), it contains no JavaScript at all.

3. Server logs. The site is hosted on a third-party static hosting service, which may keep access logs (such as time of access, IP address and user agent) under its own rules. Those logs are handled by the provider under its privacy policy; the brand does not read them, does not use them to identify individuals and does not build profiles.

4. External links. This site may link to third-party platforms. Their privacy practices are their own responsibility; the brand does not control and is not responsible for them.

5. Contacting us. If you contact us proactively through an official channel, the information you provide is used only to answer your enquiry or discuss partnership, and is not used for any other purpose or shared externally.

6. Minors. This site contains brand and wellness knowledge content and collects no information from minors.

7. Updates. Any change to this notice will be made on this page with the date below amended. No separate notification is issued.

! Compliance notice: this notice applies to this site only and makes no statement about the practices of any third party.
